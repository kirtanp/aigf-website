Please read carefully the guiding principles for the discussions at the AI Governance Forum.

- The duration of each talk will be 15 minutes followed by 5 minutes for questions and discussions. The duration for the panelists may vary and can be determined by the moderator.
- Please be kind and considerate to each other. The forum is a co-creation exercise grounded in collective intelligence to advance ideas, concepts, and paradigms about AI Governance.
- Please respect the time and the rhythm. Whether it is time to speak or ask questions, it is essential to respect the timing for the forum to be fruitful for all. The moderators will help with time.
- The objective of the forum is to establish collaborations which can guide and shape the agenda of future discourses in AI Governance and other relevant themes.
