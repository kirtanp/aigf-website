**Martin Jaggi** is an Associate Professor at EPFL, heading the Machine Learning and Optimization Laboratory. His research focuses on large language models and collaborative AI systems. Before joining EPFL, he was a post-doctoral researcher at ETH Zurich, at the Simons Institute in Berkeley, and at École Polytechnique in Paris. He has earned his PhD in Machine Learning and Optimization from ETH Zurich in 2011, and a MSc in Mathematics also from ETH Zurich. He is a member of the steering committee of the Swiss AI initiative, a co-founder of EPFL’s Applied Machine Learning Days, and a Fellow of the European ELLIS network.
