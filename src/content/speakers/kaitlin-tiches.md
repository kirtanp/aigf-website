**Kaitlin Tiches** is the embedded Librarian and Knowledge Manager for the Digital Wellness Lab at Boston Children’s Hospital, a research team with projects focused on the intersection of youth mental and physical health and their interactions with digital media. She frequently plans community presentations which provide evidence-based presentations to parents, educators, and the wider community to support children to engage with media in ways which support their mental and physical health.
