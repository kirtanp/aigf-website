**Robert Praas** is Data Scientist at CEPS. Previously, he worked in the Global Governance, Regulation, Innovation and Digital Economy (GRID) unit at CEPS. He used to be a Research Assistant at the Impact Centre Erasmus and a Thesis intern at the Medical University of Vienna.

Robert holds a master degree in Machine Learning from KTH Royal Institute of Technology and bachelor degrees in Econometrics and Operations Research & Economics and Business Economics from Erasmus University Rotterdam.
