**Orian Dheu** is a doctoral candidate and research associate at the Centre for IT & IP Law of KU Leuven (Belgium).

Between 2019 and 2022, he was a Marie-Curie fellow participating in the European Safer Autonomous Systems (SAS) ITN project where he focused his research on the legal challenges raised by autonomous systems, with the emphasis put on the civil liability questions.

He currently studies the civil liability implications of AI and related applications. He holds a Bachelor’s degree in General Law (LLB) and a first year of Master’s degree (maitrise) in Private Law from the University of Paris 1 Panthéon-Sorbonne (France). He then specialized with a Master’s degree (LLM) in Air Law and Management from Aix-Marseille University (France).
