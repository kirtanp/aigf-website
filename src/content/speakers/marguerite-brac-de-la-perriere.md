**Marguerite Brac de La Perrière**, associate, provides highly operational legal and strategic support in the fields of digital health, personal data protection and health law.

Marguerite Brac de La Perrière has a strong expertise in digital health, in health regulations and data protection, in particular health data, as well as in IT contracts.

After having managed the activities of Orange Healthcare, and having created and managed the digital health department of Alain Bensoussan Lexing for 10 years, Marguerite Brac de La Perrière joined Lerins & BCW at the end of 2020 as an IT DATA partner.
