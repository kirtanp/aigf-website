**Merve Hickok** is the founder of **[AIethicist.org](http://www.aiethicist.org/)**. She provides consultancy and training services to private & public organizations on Responsible AI development, use and governance. Her work focuses on AI bias, impact of AI systems on fundamental rights, and social justice. Merve is a Data Ethics Lecturer at University of Michigan. She is also Research Director at Center for AI & Digital Policy. The Center educates AI policy practitioners and advocates across ~50 countries. Merve has been recognized by a number of organizations – most recently as one of the _100 Brilliant Women in AI Ethics™ – 2021_, and as _Runner-up for Responsible AI Leader of the Year – 2022 (Women in AI)_.
