**Maria Axente** is an acknowledged AI ethics and policy expert. As PwC’s Head of AI Public Policy and Ethics, she is responsible for integrating ethical and policy considerations into the company’s AI strategies, promoting partnerships with key stakeholders, and leading PwC’s engagement in public policy debates and initiatives. Previously she was PwC Responsible AI lead, pioneering the adoption of ethical AI practices for the UK firm and its clients.. Additionally, Maria champions AI for children and human centric AI.
