**Maty Bohacek** is a student at Stanford University and student researcher at Google DeepMind. Advised by [Prof. Hany Farid](https://farid.berkeley.edu/) and [Prof. Maneesh Agrawala](https://graphics.stanford.edu/~maneesh/), he works at the intersection of AI, computer vision, and media forensics. His goal is to build AI systems that are inherently trustworthy and interpretable.
