**Jamie Berryhill** is an artificial intelligence (AI) analyst and project lead at the OECD.AI Policy Observatory, a team working with governments to help the manage risks and reap the benefits of AI through new approaches to policy, regulation and international collaboration. Before this, he served as an Innovation Specialist at the OECD Observatory of Public Sector Innovation (OPSI), helping governments to understand, test, and embed new ways of doing things. In his pre-OECD days, he led the White House digital government policy team and conducted data-driven program reviews for the US Congress at the Government Accountability Office (GAO).

He is passionate about positively transforming economies, societies, and governments through innovation and technology.
